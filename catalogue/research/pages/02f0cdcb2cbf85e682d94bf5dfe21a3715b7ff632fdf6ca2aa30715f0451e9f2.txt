[description]
Welecome to INDIEWEB.SOCIAL

A Mastodon instance focused on all things #OpenWeb: including the #Indieweb movement,the #Fediverse, #Mastodon Social, #Selfsovereign #identity (#SSI), #Humanetech design and #Calm technologies and more.

Discussing, Promoting, and Building

This instance will be a home to others focused on this, an amplifier to key voices across the open web and open social spaces, as well as over time, a lab to implement early experiments on how such technologies can be optimized and implemented on the #Mastodon platform.

We are committed to the <a href="https://joinmastodon.org/covenant">Mastodon Server Covenant.</a>

[server rule 1]
Be Respectful
Keep conversations civil and respectful, even in disagreement.

[server rule 2]
No Inauthentic Behaviour
Spam, unmarked bot accounts, unmarked parody accounts, impersonation, excessive self promotion, or excessive reporting.

[server rule 3]
No Hate Speech
Pejorative or discriminatory language based on religion, ethnicity, nationality, race, colour, descent, gender or other identity factor.

[server rule 4]
No Abuse, Harassment or Violence
Threats, degrading statements, targeted deadnaming, targeted misgendering, or the promotion of harmful behaviour, violence or violent ideologies.

[server rule 5]
No Adult Content
Pornography, sexually explicit, graphically violent, or uncensored disturbing content.

[server rule 6]
No Illegal Content
As set out by the UK Online Safety Act.

[server rule 7]
No Misinformation/Fake News
Knowingly sharing false or misleading information.

[server rule 8]
No Trolling or Bullying
Posting solely to offend, annoy, or cause argument.
