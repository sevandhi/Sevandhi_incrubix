FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Launch Llama Newsletter.

What is Launch Llama Newsletter?

Launch Llama Newsletter is a free weekly newsletter and directory for founders, indie hackers, and developers who want to discover the best AI tools, developer tools, and SaaS products to ship faster. Every Tuesday, we curate the most useful tools and resources for builders.

How do I submit a product to the Launch Llama Newsletter tool directory?

You can submit your AI tool, SaaS product, or developer tool directly to the Launch Llama tool directory at tools.launchllama.co. It's free to list your product and get it in front of 55,000+ founders and developers. Simply visit the directory, click Submit a Tool, and fill in your product details. Featured tools may also be highlighted in an upcoming Launch Llama Newsletter issue.

How often is Launch Llama Newsletter published?

Launch Llama Newsletter is published every Tuesday. Each issue features hand-picked AI tools, dev tools, no-code/low-code platforms, and startup resources — all designed to help founders and developers move faster.

Is Launch Llama Newsletter free to subscribe to?

Subscribing to Launch Llama Newsletter is completely free. Just enter your email and hit Subscribe. You'll get our next Tuesday issue delivered straight to your inbox, packed with the best tools for builders.

Is the Launch Llama Newsletter only for technical founders and developers?

While Launch Llama Newsletter is especially popular with developers and technical founders, it's a great read for anyone building a startup or product in the AI and SaaS space. Whether you're a solo founder, product manager, or early-stage team, our curated AI tools and dev tools will help you move faster.

How does Launch Llama Newsletter select which AI tools to feature?

Our editorial team reviews hundreds of AI tools, SaaS products, and developer utilities each week. We look for tools that are genuinely useful, well-built, and solve real problems for founders. Only the best make it into a Launch Llama Newsletter issue — no filler, no fluff.

Can I advertise or sponsor an issue of Launch Llama Newsletter?

Yes — Launch Llama Newsletter offers sponsored newsletter placements to reach 55,000+ builders, founders, and developers. Sponsorships are ideal for AI tool companies, SaaS products, and developer platforms looking to grow their user base fast. Visit our Get Featured page to learn more.

How many subscribers does Launch Llama Newsletter have?

Launch Llama Newsletter is read by 55,000+ subscribers every week. Our audience includes founders, indie hackers, software engineers, and product builders — with readers at top companies like Google, Stripe, and Notion. It's one of the fastest-growing newsletters focused on AI tools and startup resources.

What makes Launch Llama Newsletter different from other tech newsletters?

Unlike broad tech newsletters, Launch Llama Newsletter is laser-focused on tools that help founders and developers ship faster. Every issue is curated specifically for builders — covering AI tools, no-code platforms, SaaS infrastructure, dev tools, and actionable startup resources. We skip the hype and focus on what actually works.

How do I unsubscribe from Launch Llama Newsletter?

You can unsubscribe from Launch Llama Newsletter at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email. We respect your inbox — no dark patterns, no guilt trips. You can also manage your preferences or pause your subscription from the link in any issue.

Does Launch Llama Newsletter feature indie hackers and bootstrapped startups?

Absolutely. Launch Llama Newsletter is a big favorite in the indie hacker and bootstrapped startup community. We feature tools that are affordable, practical, and founder-friendly — not just enterprise software. If you're building solo or with a small team, Launch Llama is built for you.
