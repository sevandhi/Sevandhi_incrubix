Frequently Asked Questions

Building something new or exploring tools on Pitchwall?
Here are some of the most common questions we get from founders, marketers, and sponsors — all in one place.

1. What is Pitchwall?

Pitchwall is a discovery platform for AI tools, SaaS products, and tech startups. It helps founders, marketers, and investors discover trending products while giving companies a powerful way to showcase what they’re building and reach a high-intent tech audience.

65K+ listed products

45K+ newsletter subscribers

100K+ active users

2. Who uses Pitchwall?

Our community includes SaaS founders, product marketers, growth teams, investors, AI enthusiasts, and tech enthusiasts looking for new tools and innovations.

3. Is Pitchwall free to use?

Yes. Browsing and discovering products is completely free. Founders can also list their products for free, with premium visibility and sponsorship options available.

4. What kind of products are accepted on Pitchwall?

Pitchwall celebrates tech innovation — from early-stage ideas to polished global products.
We welcome submissions from makers building in the AI, SaaS, open-source, and broader tech space. Whether you’ve built a productivity app, a developer tool, or a clever AI project on GitHub — if it helps people work smarter, we want to see it.

To keep Pitchwall focused and valuable for our audience, here’s what we don’t accept:

Service-based businesses (like digital marketing agencies, web developers, or design studios)

Non-tech or non-product listings

Landing pages without a working demo or product presence

That said, you don’t need to be “fully launched” to be featured.
Even Beta or Alpha-stage products are welcome — as long as you have a clear landing page, clean UI/UX, and concise messaging about what your product does.

Pitchwall is about discovery — so if you’re building something new, useful, and tech-driven, you belong here.

5. How do you verify listed products?

Every submission is manually reviewed to ensure the product is legitimate, active, and relevant to our audience before being approved.

6. How can I list my product on Pitchwall?

Listing your product on Pitchwall is simple — just head over to the Submit Product page, share your details, and let our team do the rest. Every submission is manually reviewed to maintain quality and relevance.

We offer two listing options:

Free Listing

Free listings go through our standard review queue and typically have a waiting period of 30+ days before publication.

Featured Listing

If you want instant visibility, choose the Featured Listing. It’s reviewed and published on your preferred launch date, giving you:

Prime placement on the homepage

A guaranteed spot in our newsletter

A Twitter mention

A do-follow backlink from your product page

Both listing types are manually approved — but featured listings get prioritized visibility across the Pitchwall ecosystem, ensuring your launch gets the attention it deserves.

7. How do you choose which products to feature in the Pitchwall newsletter?

Our editorial team selects products based on innovation, usefulness, and market relevance. We also feature sponsored products that align well with our readers’ interests.

8. Can I promote my product on Pitchwall?

Yes! We offer multiple promotion opportunities, including:

Newsletter sponsorships (45K+ subscribers)

Homepage ad placements

Branded content and blog integrations

9. What kind of results can I expect from a Pitchwall promotion?

Sponsors and featured products often see a noticeable spike in qualified traffic, newsletter signups, and brand recognition. Many report ROI-driven visibility, particularly for early-stage SaaS tools.

10. Do you provide performance analytics for sponsors?

Yes. Every sponsored campaign comes with a post-campaign performance report, including impressions, clicks, and engagement insights based on our ESP data and analytics.

11. Can I update my product details after listing?

Absolutely! You can log in using the same email ID you used while submitting your product and edit your listing anytime — whether you’ve launched new features, updated pricing, or simply want to refresh your visuals or messaging.

Your changes will go through a quick review to ensure consistency, and once approved, your updated listing will go live on Pitchwall.

If you face any issue while updating, just drop us a note at support@pitchwall.co — our team will help right away.

12. What’s the audience profile of Pitchwall’s newsletter?

Our readers are mainly founders, growth marketers, SaaS professionals, tech and AI enthusiasts, and investors interested in new tools and digital trends.
It’s one of the most engaged B2B audiences in the tech discovery space.

13. How do I get featured as a sponsor?

You can email us directly at support@pitchwall.co or fill out our Sponsor Inquiry Form.
Our team will guide you through the available slots, formats, and performance options to help your brand get maximum visibility.
