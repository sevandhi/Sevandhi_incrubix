Small Business Owners

[community sidebar]
A community for Small Business Owners and those who want to be.  This place is for people to network and get advice about starting and running a small business.

Helpful Resources:
- US Small Business Association https://www.sba.gov/counseling/local-assistance/

Rules
- This is for the business aspect, not a place to gauge interest in a business idea.
- Keep it positive.  If you have to tell someone something is bad, please make sure you give an alternative or talk about why it isn't a good idea.
- Please mention in your post title where you are located.  Different laws and regulations for different areas, so this helps in making sure you get the best information possible
- If you use AI for your post or comment, please declare it.  There are still people who don't trust AI, and those who do.  
- Business plans are welcomed, but more for formatting and structure.
