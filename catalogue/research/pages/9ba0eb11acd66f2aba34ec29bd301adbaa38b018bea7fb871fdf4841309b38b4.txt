[category: Beginners]
Use this category for any basic question you have on any of the Hugging Face  library. Don’t moderate yourself, everyone has to begin somewhere and everyone on this forum is here to help!

[category: Intermediate]
Use this category for any advanced question you have on any of the Hugging Face  library or to share/coordinate with other users your projects using them.

Use this category for any research question or to coordinate on a project with other users.
