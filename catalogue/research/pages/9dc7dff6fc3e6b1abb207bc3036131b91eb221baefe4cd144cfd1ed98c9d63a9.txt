**English**

Your registration request will be processed more quickly if you introduce yourself on [the forum](https://foro.hardlimit.com/en). Tell us who you are, what interests you, and why you want to register on our Peertube instance. [Sign up](https://foro.hardlimit.com/en/register) for the forum and post your introduction in the General category.


The official support channel is [on our forum](https://foro.hardlimit.com/en/topic/63363/).

Some of your videos might be chosen to be added to our forum. When that happens, @hial-9000@foro.hardlimit.com will leave a comment with a summary of the video.

This is a technology issue instance.
Please, keep it clean of political, religious and other non-technology related videos.
If you want to upload non-technology educational videos or tech educational videos in a language different than Spanish or English, please, go to [TubEdu](https://tubedu.org)

**We are a community. We are NOT a cloud storage service.**  That means there are certain rules in order for you to use our resources:

**1)** Not listed/private videos are not welcome. You should try to make your videos public so anybody can enjoy them.

**2)** If you want to share huge files (ie. several hours 4k videos), even if they are public, that must be high quality content.

**3)** Do not use this instance to mass-import videos from YT channels that are not yours.

**4)** If you are going to import a huge amount of videos from your Youtube channel, please, contact us first.

**5)** This instance is intended for you to share your videos with others. Don't use it as a storage/backup service. Even though we try our best, data integrity/availability is not guaranteed over time. Always keep a local backup of your videos.

Please, contact us if you have any question.
