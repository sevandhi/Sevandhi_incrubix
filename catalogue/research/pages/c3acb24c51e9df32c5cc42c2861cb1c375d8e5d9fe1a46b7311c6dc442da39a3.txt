Self-Promotion and Solicitation

[first post]
The DeepLearning.AI Forum is a valuable resource for learning and sharing knowledge about AI. To maintain a focused and helpful environment, we ask that users limit self-promotion and solicitation.

Posting Frequency & Guidelines

Companies: You may create one post per month in the “AI Discussions” category.

If you have updates, you must update your existing thread rather than creating a new topic.

Posts must be informative, on-brand, and relevant to the community.

Self-Promotion of Projects: You may share your personal projects once a week.

If you are posting about the same project or “topic,” you must keep all updates in the same thread. Creating new topics for the same project is not permitted.

Job Postings: You may post relevant openings in “AI Discussions” using the “Careers” tag.

User Reporting: Users are encouraged to report suspicious listings, which are then reviewed by human teams for removal.

Not Allowed

Self-Promotion in Responses: Mentors and users are not allowed to promote services or external ventures within Forum responses.

Solicitation: Avoid directly soliciting users for business. Offer helpful advice first to build your reputation.

Promoting Other Forums: The DeepLearning.AI Forum is our central hub; please do not invite users to other online forums.

Github & Open Source Projects: * You are welcome to invite users to collaborate on Github by sharing repository links for technical discussions.

You must not “lure” or transition the core conversation, troubleshooting, or educational Q&A away from the DeepLearning.AI Forum to external chat groups or private platforms.
