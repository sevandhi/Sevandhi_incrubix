[instance name]
PeerTube.wtf

[short description]
A PeerTube provider for general use, hosted in northern Europe.

[description]
Welcome to WTF's PeerTube platform. This platform uses the official [Search Index](https://instances.joinpeertube.org) to allow users to look up content from over a thousand PeerTube platforms.

[terms]
- This video platform is for sharing videos. Uploading video as **unlisted** or **private** will eventually lead to the video being deleted.

- You may not upload copyrighted content, unless permission is given or is considered **fair use**.

- If you wish to synchronize your entire YouTube channel, you are required to promote your PeerTube channel on your socials. This includes YouTube. Being active on PeerTube is also a requirement.

Remember, PeerTube.wtf is not a backup solution for your videos. This is a video sharing platform and community.

[code of conduct]
1. No bigotry - including racism, sexism, ableism, homophobia, transphobia, or xenophobia.
2. Be respectful. Everyone should feel welcome here.
3. Uploading pornography or gore is forbidden.
4. Uploading or posting content that is illegal in Denmark, is prohibited.
5. No incitement of violence or promotion of violent ideologies.
6. No spam.

*If you see something against the rules, please report it.*

[moderation information]
- Uploading gore or pornography is **not** allowed.
- A NSFW video, must be tagged as such. If not, it will be deleted without warning.
