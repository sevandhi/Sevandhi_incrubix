Where and how to launch your product to get your first users: a step-by-step playbook with a free checklist, the best launch day, and how to promote your launch. Uneed is a free Product Hunt alternative.

Launching your product is one of the fastest ways to get visibility, feedback, and your first users. This guide covers where and how to launch your product on Uneed — from preparing before launch day to promoting it everywhere — so you get the most out of your launch. Whether it's your first launch or your tenth, here's the complete playbook.

Before Launch Day: How to Prepare

Build anticipation with a pre-launch page

Before your official launch, share your pre-launch page to collect emails from interested users. They'll be notified when your product goes live, giving you an instant boost of traffic and votes on launch day.

Tips:

Share your pre-launch page on social media

Add a link to your pre-launch page in your email signature

Reach out to relevant communities where your target users hang out

Prepare your communication materials

Don't wait until launch day to create your visuals and posts. A solid product launch strategy includes preparing in advance:

Eye-catching images for social media (X/Twitter, LinkedIn, etc.)

A compelling launch announcement post

A short video or GIF showing your product in action

An email draft to notify your existing users and newsletter subscribers

Choose the right launch day

Timing matters. Consider these factors:

Avoid weekends and holidays — activity is generally lower

Tuesday to Thursday works best for most products

Check if any major event might overshadow your launch

Want to pick your exact launch date? Skip the waiting line and choose the day that works best for you

Optimize Your Product Page

Perfect your logo, title, and description

Your product listing is the first thing people see. Make it count:

Logo: Use a clear, recognizable logo that stands out at small sizes

Title: Keep it short and memorable — it should instantly convey what your product does

Description: Explain your product simply and clearly. Focus on the problem you solve and the benefits, not just features

Display a "Launching Soon" badge

Add a "Launching Soon on Uneed" badge to your website — you can get it from your product page. It builds anticipation and shows visitors that something exciting is coming.

Grab the 'Launching Soon on Uneed' badge from your product page.

Create a deal for Uneed users

Offering an exclusive discount for Uneed users is a great way to:

Attract more clicks to your product page

Get a special "Deal" badge displayed next to your product in rankings

Convert visitors into paying customers

Go to your product page to create a deal with a coupon code or special offer.

Launch Day Strategy: How to Get Votes

Turn it into an event

The platform is just a starting point — what really matters is your ability to turn your launch into an event. People love supporting makers during their launch, and if you create excitement:

Your impressions on social media will increase

Your click-through rates will be higher

More people will engage with your product

Be present and active

On launch day:

Be available to respond quickly to comments and questions

Thank every supporter who votes or comments on your product

Share updates throughout the day on social media

Engage with other products launching the same day — the community appreciates makers who support each other

This is where the Uneed Pro community changes the game. Inside the private Discord, founders and Uneed Residency alumni rally around each other's launches — real-time feedback, upvotes, and honest advice from people who've shipped before. Launching alone is hard; launching with a room full of founders behind you is a different story.

Share your story

People connect with stories, not just products. Share:

Why you built this product

The challenges you faced along the way

What makes your journey unique

Authenticity resonates with the community and makes people more likely to support you.

Cross-promote everywhere

Don't rely only on Uneed traffic. Share your launch on:

X/Twitter with relevant hashtags

LinkedIn (especially for B2B products)

Reddit (find relevant subreddits, but follow their rules!)

IndieHackers

Your own email list

Discord/Slack communities you're part of
